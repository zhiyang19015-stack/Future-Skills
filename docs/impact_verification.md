The unit of KPI 2 changed with the rewrite: Phase 1 counted 186 skills of our own list for 25 IT roles; this version uses O*NET's skills and knowledge areas, weighted by how important each is to the occupation. Both count a skill only when a free resource for it has a link we opened and dated.

An earlier version reported a career-clarity result from 20 testers. Its response sheet does not support that number, so it is withdrawn and not used here.

## What KPI 1 needs from the product, checked on synthetic people

For a person to name a target role, its missing skills and a next course, the app must give all three. Run on 96 synthetic people (the 5 demo people and a working adult in every 10th occupation, with only their job, field and usual education), the app gave a target occupation, at least two skill gaps and a checked free course for **96 of 96 (100%)**. This tests the system, not people: it is the ceiling KPI 1 can reach, not a result for KPI 1.
