- **Bookkeeping, Accounting, and Auditing Clerks** (clerical): Highest ILO gradient and measured AI use; the lead case for automation-leaning tasks
- **Data Entry Keyers** (clerical): ILO gradient 4 (data entry); checks that an exposed job is not shown as disappearing
- **Customer Service Representatives** (clerical): Service by phone and chat; AI agents in the news scenarios
- **Secretaries and Administrative Assistants, Except Legal, Medical, and Executive** (clerical): Office support with mixed tasks
- **Accountants and Auditors** (professional): MyCOL-listed profession with high observed AI use
- **Software Developers** (professional): Builds AI and uses it heavily; the two sources may disagree
- **Lawyers** (professional): Regulated profession (licence) with drafting and research tasks
- **Elementary School Teachers, Except Special Education** (professional): Teaching: augmentation rather than automation expected
- **Cooks, Restaurant** (service): Manual service work little seen in chat-AI data
- **Childcare Workers** (service): Care work; human-led tasks expected
- **Retail Salespersons** (service): Sales floor work in a declining DOSM group
- **Team Assemblers** (manufacturing): Its ISCO-08 group has no ILO score; checks the missing-data path
- **Machinists** (manufacturing): Machine work: robots are not in the AI-use data
- **Inspectors, Testers, Sorters, Samplers, and Weighers** (manufacturing): Inspection with some data tasks
- **Registered Nurses** (health): MyCOL-listed and regulated; clinical scribes news scenario
- **Nursing Assistants** (health): Hands-on care with little AI use
- **Pharmacy Technicians** (health): Pharmacy records and dispensing
- **Electricians** (trades): Construction trade; safety programme with unknown fees
- **Automotive Service Technicians and Mechanics** (trades): Repair trade with diagnostic software
- **Carpenters** (trades): Manual trade with almost no AI use
